7 · Why this calendar

Close 2026 with GREATMAN Wolsztyn 70.3 (B-race, 12 days post-Malbork DNF). Malbork was a mechanical DNF at ~45 km, not a finished IM — no transition unload. Week 1 resumes quality (anaerobic bike hole + swim volume) then eases into a mini-taper. Residual IM fitness is enough for a controlled ~4:20 70.3. Then winter base, spring 70.3-specific build into Garmin Syców (B-peak, stretch 3:59), one-week reset, then IM-specific 4h+2h bricks and a 2-week taper into Barcelona (A-goal 8:50). Syców is a real race, not a workout — mini-taper, race-power openers — but the bike is capped at ~260 W NP so the run can hold ~3:36/km and Barcelona's 2:59 is not spent in July. Swim limiter is cold/OW skill not CSS; hold >=3 swims/week.

Progressive overload, bounded

Load grows peak-to-peak at ≤10% TSS/week between load weeks (recovery valleys excluded); projected CTL ramp stays under the 7/week cap. Starting CTL ~69.4. A recovery valley (W7) and the first-race deload keep ACWR under 1.5.

Swim, the limiter

Three swims every week regardless of phase. Frequency > duration for a thin swim base: it builds feel-for-water and durability without stealing the bike/run hours that move the clock more.

Bike: convert the watts

Long rides are on the TT bike in race position so the new disc/aero setup becomes a real race split, not a dyno number. Race-power long rides rehearse IM pacing and fuelling (70-90 g carb/h).

Run: durability over speed

The IM result lives or dies on the run. Volume is aerobic (Z2) with race-effort finishes and brick runs off long rides so the legs learn IM marathon pace under fatigue — never a full-distance run.

Taper & race-day form

Two-week taper holds intensity but cuts volume so fatigue (ATL) sheds faster than fitness (CTL). The validator projects race-day TSB in the +10..+25 band that maximizes A-race performance without going stale.
