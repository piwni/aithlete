7 · Why this calendar

Two-peak season. Syców 1/2 IM (2026-07-12) is a B-race tune-up off a 5-day mini-taper; the A-race is Malbork full IM (2026-09-06). Block order: sharpen (W1-2) -> Syców -> reset (W4) -> IM build (W5-9 with a recovery valley W7) -> 2-week taper. Swim is the limiter (CTL 4.1, ~6h/8wk) so swim freq is held at >=3/wk throughout. Bike volume is on the TT bike in race position to convert the new disc/aero setup (~20-30 W) into a free IM bike split. The IM block runs 4h+2h race-simulation bricks (W6/W8 4.0h ride + 2.0h IM-pace run, W9 4.5h + 2.0h) in place of a standalone long run those weeks — the athlete has used 3+2 bricks durably before and the run is the A-race lever, so we rehearse race legs directly. Peak weekly volume is pushed toward the 16-17h ceiling (W9 ~16.5h) while staying inside the ramp/ACWR guardrails.

Progressive overload, bounded

Load grows peak-to-peak at ≤10% TSS/week between load weeks (recovery valleys excluded); projected CTL ramp stays under the 7/week cap. Starting CTL ~64.4. A recovery valley (W7) and the first-race deload keep ACWR under 1.5.

Swim, the limiter

Three swims every week regardless of phase. Frequency > duration for a thin swim base: it builds feel-for-water and durability without stealing the bike/run hours that move the clock more.

Bike: convert the watts

Long rides are on the TT bike in race position so the new disc/aero setup becomes a real race split, not a dyno number. Race-power long rides rehearse IM pacing and fuelling (70-90 g carb/h).

Run: durability over speed

The IM result lives or dies on the run. Volume is aerobic (Z2) with race-effort finishes and brick runs off long rides so the legs learn IM marathon pace under fatigue — never a full-distance run.

Taper & race-day form

Two-week taper holds intensity but cuts volume so fatigue (ATL) sheds faster than fitness (CTL). The validator projects race-day TSB in the +10..+25 band that maximizes A-race performance without going stale.
